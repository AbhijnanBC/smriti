To isolate spurious cross-domain contradiction from genuine contradiction
detection, we drew a separate sample of 60 candidate pairs in which one
claim comes from a deliberately-planted adversarial document (B11/C11/D11)
and the other comes from an \emph{unrelated} document elsewhere in the
corpus (not the document it was written to contradict) --- pairs that should
essentially always be NEUTRAL. SMRITI classified 11 of these 60 pairs
(18.3\%) as CONTRADICTS. Both independent annotation passes agreed that
\textbf{all 11} (100\%) were false positives --- none was a genuine
contradiction. This is a clean, isolated confirmation of the mechanism: the
NLI classifier is not simply noisy at the margins of genuine contradiction
detection, it is actively misfiring on candidate pairs that embedding-based
retrieval surfaced as similar but that have no real semantic relationship
to contradict at all.
