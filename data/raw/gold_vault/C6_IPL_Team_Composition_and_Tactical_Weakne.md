# C6. IPL Team Composition and Tactical Weakness Analysis

**Source:** ABP Live

RCB's squad composition for 2026 highlights some structural issues. While the batting order, anchored by Virat Kohli and Phil Salt, looks formidable, the bowling unit lacks depth in both spin and pace. The all-rounder department, featuring Krunal Pandya and Tim David, provides balance but is not a reliable bowling option.

The reliance on a few key bowlers makes RCB vulnerable to injuries or loss of form. Additionally, the lack of a genuine wicket-taking spinner in the middle overs could allow opposing teams to build big partnerships, putting pressure on the batting lineup to chase high totals.
