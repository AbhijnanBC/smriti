# C10. IPL 2026 Team Squad Analysis and Key Players

**Source:** ABP Live

Royal Challengers Bengaluru's projected 2026 squad includes:

- **Batting Core:** Virat Kohli, Phil Salt, Rajat Patidar.
- **All-rounders:** Krunal Pandya, Tim David.
- **Bowling Unit:** Josh Hazlewood, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Suyash Sharma, Yash Dayal.

The team has a good blend of experience and youth, but the lack of a reliable fifth bowler could be a concern. The management will hope that the young bowlers step up and that the senior players stay fit throughout the tournament. The success of RCB in IPL 2026 will largely depend on how well their bowling unit performs under pressure.
