# C1. IPL 2026: RCB's Defensive Championship Challenge

**Source:** ABP Live

The defending champions, Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB), enter the IPL 2026 season with the heavy weight of expectation. Despite a star-studded batting lineup featuring Virat Kohli and Phil Salt, the Bengaluru outfit faces two glaring tactical vulnerabilities that could derail their title defence.

**Lack of Wrist-Spin Depth:** In the modern T20 era, the middle overs are won or lost by the ability to take wickets with wrist spin. While RCB has secured the services of Suyash Sharma to lead their leg-spin attack, the cupboard looks remarkably bare beyond him. The support cast offers decent finger-spin but none possess the game-changing "mystery" or wicket-taking threat of a specialist wrist spinner. This lack of depth means the opposition batters can target the spinners without fear of a variety of bowling styles.

**Injury-Prone Pace Battery:** RCB's fast bowling unit, led by Josh Hazlewood and Bhuvneshwar Kumar, brings immense pedigree but also carries a history of fitness concerns. Hazlewood has been plagued by recurring injuries, while Kumar has struggled with consistency. Relying on a pace attack where the primary strike bowlers are either returning from long layoffs or managing recurring niggles is a high-stakes gamble. If even one of them breaks down mid-season, the back-up options are untested at the IPL level.

These weaknesses could be exploited by teams with strong spin resources, making RCB's title defence a challenging one.
