# C3. Tactical Role of Wrist-Spinners in T20 Cricket

**Source:** CricToday / Glance

Leg-spinners bowling in the middle overs (overs 11-40) of limited-overs cricket enjoy bowling during these phases because the field restrictions are relaxed, and they can use the pitch conditions to their advantage. Senior India spinner Yuzvendra Chahal has continued to prove his dominance in the middle overs, underlining the value of wrist spin in T20 cricket.

The key role of spinners is to take wickets while keeping the run rate under control. On surfaces that offer grip, wrist-spinners can be particularly effective, as their variations are harder to read. Their effectiveness often depends on the support of a good fielding captain who sets aggressive field placements to create pressure.
