# B4. WGAN-GP Applications in Image Restoration and Fault Diagnosis

**Source:** ScienceDirect / Research Papers

WGAN-GP has also been adapted for image restoration tasks. A dynamic multi-scale WGAN-GP framework combines colorization and image denoising, addressing the limitations of distinct restoration models by learning a joint mapping from degraded images to clean ones.

In industrial fault diagnosis, an improved WGAN-GP is used to generate diverse fault data for dataset augmentation. Since fault data are often scarce, the augmented dataset enables more robust training of classification models. The WGAN-GP learns the distribution of fault signatures and generates realistic samples that cover various fault types, improving diagnostic accuracy.
