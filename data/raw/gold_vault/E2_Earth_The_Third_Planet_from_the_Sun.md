# E2. Earth: The Third Planet from the Sun

**Source:** Wikipedia

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the densest and fifth-largest of the eight planets. It is the largest of the Solar System's four terrestrial planets. Earth is the only known planet to have large amounts of liquid water on its surface, and it is the only astronomical object known to harbour life.

Earth orbits the Sun at an average distance of about 149.6 million kilometres (93 million miles), taking 365.25 days to complete one revolution. Its axial tilt of 23.44 degrees causes the annual seasons. The planet's surface is divided into tectonic plates that drift over geological time, creating continents and ocean basins.
