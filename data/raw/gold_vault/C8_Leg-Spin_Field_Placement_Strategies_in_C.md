# C8. Leg-Spin Field Placement Strategies in Cricket

**Source:** CricToday

Here are some of the most typically used field setups wrist-spinners use to set up a batter and be economical:

- **Leg slip and short leg:** Used for the leg-break, aiming for a catch off the edge.
- **Silly point and slip:** For the googly, attacking the off-stump line.
- **Long-on and deep mid-wicket:** For the loopy delivery that invites the big hit.
- **Cover and mid-off:** To prevent the drive through the off-side.

These field placements are designed to create pressure and force the batter to take risks. The effectiveness of the leg-spinner often depends on the captain's ability to set the right fields and the bowler's ability to bowl to them.
