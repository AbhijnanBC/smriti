# C9. The Rise of Left-Arm Wrist-Spinners in IPL

**Source:** Apex Football

Wrist-spinners like Kuldeep Yadav and Noor Ahmad, armed with variations and stifling accuracy, are turning out to be a thriving force. Their styles may be contrasting, but their role is similar: to go in search of wickets and be the X-factors for their teams.

Left-arm wrist-spinners are particularly effective against right-handed batters because the ball turns away from them, making it difficult to play against the spin. Their ability to bowl the googly (turning into the right-hander) and the conventional leg-break makes them a constant threat.
