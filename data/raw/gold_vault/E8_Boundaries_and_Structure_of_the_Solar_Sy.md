# E8. Boundaries and Structure of the Solar System

**Source:** Wikipedia

The Solar System's boundaries are defined by several markers:

- **Kuiper Belt:** A region beyond Neptune containing many icy bodies, extending from about 30 to 50 AU.
- **Kuiper Cliff:** A sudden drop in the number of objects at about 50 AU, possibly indicating the edge of the Kuiper Belt.
- **Heliopause:** The boundary where the solar wind meets the interstellar medium, located at about 120 AU. Beyond this lies the Oort Cloud, a spherical reservoir of comets extending up to 100,000 AU.

The termination shock, the heliosheath, and the heliopause are the main features of the heliosphere -- the bubble blown by the solar wind into the interstellar medium. The boundary structures define the zone influenced by the Sun's magnetic field and solar wind.
