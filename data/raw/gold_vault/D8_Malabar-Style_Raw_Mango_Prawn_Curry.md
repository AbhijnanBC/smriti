# D8. Malabar-Style Raw Mango Prawn Curry

**Source:** Tasty Malabar Foods

In the Malabar region of Kerala, this curry is well-known for its tangy and spicy taste. The raw mango (locally called panchamanga) is added to provide the sourness that makes the curry irresistible.

**Ingredients:**
- Prawns -- 500 g
- Raw mango -- 1 medium, cut into wedges
- Coconut -- 1 cup, grated
- Shallots -- 10, sliced
- Ginger -- 1 inch
- Garlic -- 6 cloves
- Red chilli powder -- 1 tbsp
- Turmeric powder -- 1/2 tsp
- Fenugreek seeds -- 1/4 tsp
- Curry leaves
- Coconut oil
- Salt

**Method:**

1. Grind the coconut with a little water to extract the first milk and keep aside.

2. Heat oil; add fenugreek seeds and curry leaves, then saute shallots, ginger, and garlic.

3. Add red chilli and turmeric powders, then add the mango wedges and prawns.

4. Add water and salt; cook until the prawns are done.

5. Add the coconut milk and simmer for 5 minutes. Serve hot.
