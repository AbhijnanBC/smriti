# E10. Earth as a Habitable Planet

**Source:** Wikipedia

One big reason life can exist on Earth is that Earth has a lot of water on its surface, being the only planet in our Solar System with liquid water. Water covers about 71% of the Earth's surface, existing in oceans, lakes, rivers, and polar ice caps.

Earth is home to millions of species, including humans. Its atmosphere provides the oxygen we breathe and protects us from harmful solar radiation. The stability of Earth's climate over billions of years has allowed life to evolve and diversify. The planet's diverse ecosystems, from rainforests to deserts, support a rich biosphere. Earth's position in the "habitable zone" -- where water can exist as a liquid -- is a key factor in its ability to sustain life.
