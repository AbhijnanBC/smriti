# D5. Raw Mango Prawn Curry -- Sanjeev Kapoor Version

**Source:** Sanjeev Kapoor

Prawns cooked in South Indian style with coconut oil lending its special flavour. The raw mango adds a tangy sourness that beautifully balances the richness of the coconut milk.

**Ingredients:**
- 500 g prawns, peeled and deveined
- 1 medium raw mango, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 2 dried red chillies
- 1/2 cup grated coconut
- 1 small onion, quartered
- 1 inch ginger, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1 cup thick coconut milk
- Salt to taste

**Method:**

1. Quarter half an onion and finely chop the remaining half.

2. Heat coconut oil in a non-stick pan; add coriander seeds, black peppercorns, and red chillies. Saute for 1 minute.

3. Add the quartered onion and grated coconut; saute for 1-2 minutes on medium heat until lightly browned.

4. Cool and transfer to a blender jar; add water, ginger, garlic, and a handful of rice (optional). Blend to a fine paste.

5. Heat remaining coconut oil in a deep pan; add the finely chopped onion and saute until translucent.

6. Add turmeric powder, then the ground paste. Saute for a few minutes until fragrant.

7. Add water and salt, bring to a boil, and cook for 1 minute on low heat.

8. Add the raw mango wedges; cover and cook for 8-10 minutes until the mango turns soft.

9. Add the prawns and coconut milk; cover and cook for 4-5 minutes until the prawns are cooked through.

10. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve with steamed rice or appam.
