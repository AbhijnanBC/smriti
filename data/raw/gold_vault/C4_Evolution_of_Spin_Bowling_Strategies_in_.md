# C4. Evolution of Spin Bowling Strategies in IPL

**Source:** Hindustan Times

Recent IPL seasons have seen a shift in how spinners are used. Grounds have reduced the gulf between wrist-spinners and finger-spinners; even spinners who cannot turn the ball both ways are thriving. The key has been control. In the middle overs, spinners have been especially successful: slower while attacking, and faster while defending, all in their own style.

The ability to bowl dot balls and build pressure is now valued as much as taking wickets. Finger-spinners like Ravichandran Ashwin and Ravindra Jadeja have adapted by varying their pace and trajectory, making them equally important in the powerplay and the death overs.
