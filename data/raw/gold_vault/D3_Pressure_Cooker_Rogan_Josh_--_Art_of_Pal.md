# D3. Pressure Cooker Rogan Josh -- Art of Palate

**Source:** Art of Palate

This recipe emphasises the use of traditional spices and a slow cooking process, even with a pressure cooker.

**Method:**

1. Melt ghee in a deep pan or pressure cooker on medium heat.

2. Add the whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon) and let them splutter.

3. Add the onion and cook until translucent. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and saute until the raw smell disappears.

4. Add the lamb and sear it until browned on all sides. Add the tomato puree and spice powders.

5. If using a pressure cooker, cover and cook on low heat until you hear about 3 whistles. Alternatively, cook on simmer for 45 minutes.

6. Once cooked, open the lid and let the curry simmer for an additional 10 minutes to thicken the gravy. Adjust seasoning and serve.
