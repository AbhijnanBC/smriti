# D4. Pressure Cooker Rogan Josh -- Key Techniques

**Source:** RecipeLand

The pressure cooker is the time-saver here, coaxing tough lamb shoulder to fork-tender in about 20 minutes instead of hours of slow braising. Once you release the pressure, simmer the curry uncovered to thicken the gravy and concentrate the flavour.

**Tips:**
- Use lamb shoulder or leg for best results; avoid lean cuts as they can become tough.
- For extra flavour, marinate the lamb in yoghurt and spices overnight.
- Adjust the spice level to your preference by varying the amount of chilli powder.
