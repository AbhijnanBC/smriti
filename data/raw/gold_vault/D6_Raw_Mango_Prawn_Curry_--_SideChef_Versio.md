# D6. Raw Mango Prawn Curry -- SideChef Version

**Source:** SideChef

This version uses a spice paste that is marinated with the prawns to intensify the flavour.

**Method:**

1. In a bowl, mix the spice paste (made from ginger, garlic, chilli, and cumin) with the green mango pieces, salt, and prawns.

2. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours.

3. Heat oil in a pan; add curry leaves and mustard seeds, then add the marinated prawn mixture.

4. Add water and cook until the prawns are cooked and the gravy thickens.

5. Finish with a dash of coconut milk and serve hot.
