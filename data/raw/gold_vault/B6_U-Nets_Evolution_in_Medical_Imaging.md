# B6. U-Net's Evolution in Medical Imaging

**Source:** IET Image Processing / Springer

Since its introduction, U-Net has spawned numerous variants, such as 3D U-Net, V-Net, Attention U-Net, and U-Net++. Each variant addresses specific challenges:

- **3D U-Net:** Extends the architecture to volumetric data (e.g., MRI and CT volumes) by replacing 2D convolutions with 3D ones.
- **Attention U-Net:** Incorporates attention gates that suppress irrelevant features, improving segmentation accuracy for objects of varying sizes.
- **U-Net++:** Adds nested and dense skip connections to reduce the semantic gap between encoder and decoder, improving performance on complex structures.

These adaptations have made U-Net the dominant approach in medical image analysis, outperforming traditional methods and even human experts in many benchmarks.
