# D9. Kerala Drumstick Raw Mango Prawns Curry

**Source:** Whisk Affair

This variation includes drumsticks (moringa pods) along with raw mango, adding a unique texture and flavour.

**Ingredients:**
- Coconut oil -- 2 tsp
- Mustard seeds -- 1/2 tsp
- Curry leaves -- 15-20
- Raw mango -- cut into wedges
- Drumstick -- peeled and cut into pieces
- Prawns -- 250 g
- Coconut milk -- 1 cup
- Green chillies, ginger, garlic paste
- Turmeric, red chilli powder

**Method:**

1. Heat coconut oil; add mustard seeds and curry leaves.

2. Add the ginger-garlic-chilli paste and saute.

3. Add the prawns, mango, drumstick, and spices; cook for 5 minutes.

4. Add coconut milk and enough water; simmer until the drumstick is cooked.

5. Season with salt and serve with rice.
