# C2. IPL 2025: The Rise of Wrist-Spinners

**Source:** Hindustan Times

Kuldeep Yadav and Noor Ahmad, both left-arm wrist spinners, have demonstrated exceptional control in the IPL. Noor Ahmad, in particular, has been a revelation, maintaining an economy of 4.7 and a dot-ball percentage of 47.3% against left-handed batters. However, both statistics drop significantly against right-handed batters, indicating that match-up tactics are crucial.

Teams are increasingly using data analytics to decide which bowler to use against specific batters, and wrist-spinners are often deployed in the middle overs to break partnerships. Their ability to turn the ball both ways and bowl multiple variations makes them valuable assets in a T20 lineup.
