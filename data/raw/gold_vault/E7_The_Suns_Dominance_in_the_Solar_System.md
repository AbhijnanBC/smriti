# E7. The Sun's Dominance in the Solar System

**Source:** Wikipedia

Over 99.86% of the Solar System's mass is located within the Sun. The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star that fuses hydrogen into helium in its core. Its gravitational pull holds the entire system together, dictating the orbits of all planets, asteroids, and comets.

The Sun's energy output, known as solar luminosity, is the primary driver of climate and weather on Earth. Solar activity, such as sunspots and solar flares, can affect space weather and have impacts on satellite operations and power grids. Understanding the Sun is crucial for understanding the Solar System's past, present, and future.
