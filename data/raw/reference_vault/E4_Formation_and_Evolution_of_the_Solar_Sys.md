# E4. Formation and Evolution of the Solar System

**Source:** Wikipedia

The Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago when a dense region of a molecular cloud collapsed under its own gravity. The collapse likely was triggered by a shock wave from a nearby supernova. The central concentration became the Sun, while the surrounding material flattened into a protoplanetary disc.

Planetesimals -- small bodies of rock and ice -- formed and collided, gradually accreting into larger bodies. Within the first few million years, the giant planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) formed, and their gravitational influence shaped the orbits of the remaining bodies. The Late Heavy Bombardment, about 4 billion years ago, delivered water and organic molecules to the inner planets, possibly seeding life on Earth.
