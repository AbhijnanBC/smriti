# D2. Lamb Rogan Josh -- Licious Version

**Source:** Licious

This version adds a freshly ground spice blend to enhance the aroma of the curry.

**Ingredients:**
- 500 g lamb, bone-in
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp asafoetida (hing)
- Whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, black pepper)
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, pureed
- Ginger-garlic paste
- Red chilli powder, turmeric, salt

**Method:**

1. Grind the whole spices to a coarse powder using a spice grinder or mortar and pestle.

2. Heat oil in a pressure cooker. Add the asafoetida and the ground spices; fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.

3. Add the sliced onion and saute until golden brown. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 2 minutes.

4. Add the tomato puree, chilli powder, turmeric, and salt. Cook until the oil separates from the masala.

5. Add the lamb and coat well with the masala. Pour in 1 cup of water, secure the lid, and cook on high pressure for 20 minutes.

6. Release pressure naturally or quickly as per preference. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve.
