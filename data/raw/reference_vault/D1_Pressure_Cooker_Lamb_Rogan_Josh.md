# D1. Pressure Cooker Lamb Rogan Josh

**Source:** Women's Weekly Food

Rogan Josh is a classic Indian curry originating from Kashmir. The name means "meat in spicy red sauce," referring to the rich, crimson colour derived from Kashmiri chillies and the fragrant spice blend. This recipe uses a pressure cooker to achieve fork-tender lamb in a fraction of the time required for slow braising.

**Ingredients:**
- 1 kg lamb shoulder, cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp ghee (clarified butter)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp Rogan Josh spice paste (or 2 tbsp store-bought)
- 400 g canned tomato puree
- 250 ml water
- 100 g baby spinach
- Fresh coriander, chopped, for garnish

**Method:**

1. Heat half the ghee in a 6-litre pressure cooker over medium-high heat. Brown the lamb in batches, turning to colour all sides. Remove and set aside.

2. Add the remaining ghee to the cooker and saute the onion until lightly browned. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.

3. Return the lamb to the cooker along with the spice paste, tomato puree, and water. Stir well to combine.

4. Secure the lid and bring the cooker to high pressure. Reduce the heat to maintain steady pressure and cook for 25 minutes.

5. Remove from heat and use the quick-release method to release the pressure. Carefully open the lid.

6. Stir in the spinach and coriander; season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with steamed rice or naan bread.
