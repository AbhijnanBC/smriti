# E9. Minor Bodies in the Solar System

**Source:** Wikipedia

The Solar System contains a vast number of minor bodies:

- **Minor planets:** Known count as of 2026 is about 1,368,528, with the vast majority being asteroids in the main belt between Mars and Jupiter.
- **Comets:** About 4,591 known comets, including both short-period and long-period comets.
- **Natural satellites:** 758 known moons orbiting planets and dwarf planets.

These minor bodies provide valuable clues about the early Solar System, as they have preserved much of the original material from the protoplanetary disc. They are also objects of interest for future exploration, resource extraction, and as potential impact hazards.
