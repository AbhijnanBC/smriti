# D10. Raw Mango Prawn Curry -- Plating and Serving

**Source:** Sanjeev Kapoor

For the garnish, take off the head and shell of the prawns, keeping the tails intact. Add salt, heat coconut oil in another non-stick pan, and saute the prawns for 2-3 minutes on each side until they turn opaque.

For serving, place a portion of cooked red rice on each serving plate, put some prawn gravy over it, and garnish with the sauteed prawns and fresh curry leaves. This presentation highlights the contrasting colours of the reddish gravy, the green mangoes, and the white prawns, making it as visually appealing as it is delicious.
