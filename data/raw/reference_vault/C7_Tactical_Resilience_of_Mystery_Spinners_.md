# C7. Tactical Resilience of Mystery Spinners in T20

**Source:** Apex Football

The tactical resilience of T20's mystery spinners continues to be a decisive factor in modern cricket. Their ability to bowl multiple variations without revealing their plans to the batter makes them invaluable assets in the middle overs. Mystery spinners, such as Varun Chakravarthy and Sunil Narine, often have an immediate impact because batters struggle to pick the ball from the hand.

Their unpredictability allows captains to use them in any phase of the innings, often breaking dangerous partnerships. As teams become more data-driven, the demand for bowlers who can consistently beat the bat is only increasing.
