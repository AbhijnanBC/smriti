# E6. Earth's Density and Mass

**Source:** Wikipedia

Earth is the densest planet in the Solar System, with an average density of 5.51 g/cm3. This high density is due to its metallic core, which is composed mainly of iron and nickel. Of the four rocky planets, Earth is the largest and most massive.

Earth's near-surface environments are the only places in the universe known to harbour life. Its atmosphere, hydrosphere, and lithosphere interact in complex cycles that maintain a habitable climate. The existence of plate tectonics and a magnetic field are believed to be critical for sustaining life over geological time.
