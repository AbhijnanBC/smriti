# E3. Earth's Orbital and Physical Characteristics

**Source:** Wikipedia

Earth is about eight light-minutes away from the Sun, which means sunlight takes about 8 minutes to reach us. The planet has a moderately eccentric orbit (eccentricity 0.0167), making the distance to the Sun vary slightly over the year. Earth's age is estimated at 4.54 billion years.

The planet's atmosphere is composed mainly of nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%), which supports life. Earth's magnetic field protects the surface from solar wind and cosmic radiation. The presence of liquid water, a stable climate, and a protective magnetic field make Earth uniquely habitable.
