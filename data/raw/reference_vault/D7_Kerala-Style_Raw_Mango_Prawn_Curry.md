# D7. Kerala-Style Raw Mango Prawn Curry

**Source:** Flavor Quotient

Ingredients:

- 250 g prawns, cleaned
- 1 small raw mango, chopped
- 1 cup thick coconut milk
- 150 g shallots, thinly sliced
- 2-inch piece ginger, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 dried red chillies
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- Salt
- Coconut oil for tempering

**Method:**

1. Grind the shallots, ginger, garlic, and red chillies into a coarse paste.

2. Heat coconut oil in a pan; add the paste and saute until fragrant.

3. Add the turmeric powder, mango pieces, and prawns; cook for 5 minutes.

4. Pour in the coconut milk and simmer for 10-12 minutes until the prawns are cooked and the gravy thickens.

5. Serve with steamed rice or appam.
